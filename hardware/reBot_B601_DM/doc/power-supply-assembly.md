# Assembling the power supply enclosure

The parts are [power-supply.md](../power-supply.md), generated from
[power-supply.assy](../power-supply.assy). These are the steps, as the vendor
published them.

They are still a document rather than data: each step's words belong on the
matching node of the ASSY file (its `description:`), and its photograph has
nowhere to go there at all - see [PARTCAD.md](../../../PARTCAD.md). Until that is
true, this is where they live.
